Questions

What is Proofhand?Proofhand replaces image puzzles on sign-up, login and contact forms with an invisible proof-of-work check: the browser solves it silently, the form carries a token, and the server verifies that token once.
Is Proofhand a CAPTCHA replacement?Yes. It does the same job as reCAPTCHA or hCaptcha without asking a real visitor to identify traffic lights, and the server-side verification call is shaped like reCAPTCHA's siteverify.
How much does Proofhand cost?There is a free tier. IntegrityRuntime Pro is $49 a month, AgentPass Pro is $99 a month, Hardware Gateway Pro is $149 a month and Enterprise is $299 a month.
Does it stop bots as well as a puzzle does?Bots that solve puzzles with paid services get through those puzzles anyway; a proof-of-work check costs a machine real computation while costing a person nothing.
Will it work with my existing form?Yes. The change is a token on the form and one verification call on the server, so the form itself does not need redesigning.
Can I charge AI crawlers instead of blocking them?Yes. Proofhand lets a site make crawlers either pay for access or stay out, instead of blocking all automation or allowing all of it.
Is Proofhand accessible?It asks nothing of the visitor beyond submitting the form, so there is no visual or motor task to fail.
